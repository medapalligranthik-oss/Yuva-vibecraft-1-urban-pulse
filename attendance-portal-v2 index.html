Help

Key terms, explained

Short and simple definitions of what you see here.

🛑

Detention line · 75%

The minimum attendance. Below it in a subject, you are in the detention zone.

🛡️

Stay-safe goal

How many of the classes still left you must attend to end the semester at 75% or more.

⭐

Star goal · 90%

The same idea for 90%. A cushion, not a requirement.

🚨

Irreversible detention

The point of no return: even attending every class left, you can't reach 75% by 29 Nov.

✂️

Skip budget

How many upcoming classes you can miss and still finish at 75%.

🧮

How we calculate

Classes come from your timetable (Mon–Fri, minus holidays). Then we solve how many of the remaining you need.

FAQ

Quick entry or Mark my days?

Quick entry: type your % from the portal. Mark my days: we assume you attended everything and you only mark the days or periods you missed — all subjects update together.

Why is my number slightly different from the portal?

Quick entry estimates attended classes from a rounded %. The portal is always the official record.

What if there is a holiday or cancelled class?

Add the date under "Holidays & extras" in the Calculator and every number updates.

Is my data stored anywhere?

No. Everything stays in your browser on this device.
